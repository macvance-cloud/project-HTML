What You Can Spot

As you travel through New Zealnd you can spot many animals from your car. Here is some easy animals you can spot: Our list features the easy to see anywhere and almost everytime.

Featured Animals


  • Feral Goats

    Feral goats are hardy animals that live in mountainous and rugged environments.

  • Red Stag

    Red stags are large deer known for their impressive antlers and reddish-brown coats.

  • Fallow Deer

    Fallow deer are recognizable by their spotted coats and distinctive antlers.

  • Kea

    Kea are intelligent alpine parrots native to New Zealand.

  • Tui

    Tui are native New Zealand birds known for their distinctive white throat feathers and complex songs.

  • Woodswallow

    Woodswallows are social birds that feed primarily on insects and are often seen in groups.
